How it works

One morning alert. Not a daily feed.

BlockLine is a local market intelligence service for small service businesses, starting in Miami. The format stays the same every day: one alert, one gap, one suggested action.

  1. One alert, once in the morning

    You receive a single alert — not a dashboard and not a stream of posts. It arrives in the morning, and that is the day’s signal.

  2. A pricing gap or a review gap

    The alert flags one local gap: a price that sits out of line with nearby shops, or a review profile that is trailing comparable businesses.

  3. One suggested action

    Each alert includes one concrete next step. It is a suggestion, not a mandate. You decide whether to use it.
